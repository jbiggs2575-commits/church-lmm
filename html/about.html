A LITTLE ABOUT US

People first. Always.

We believe church is more than a Sunday morning. It’s a community learning to live with faith, hope, and a whole lot of grace.

OUR STORY

There’s a place for you at the table.

Living Manna Ministries is a neighborhood church for people at every stage of faith. Some of us have been around church for years; some are just starting to ask questions. All of us are still growing.

We gather to worship, learn from Jesus, and remind each other that none of us has to do life alone. Then we carry that care beyond our walls and into the community we call home.
